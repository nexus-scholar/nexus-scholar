# Nexus Scholar

Laravel/PHP research tooling for systematic literature reviews, scholarly search, citation networks, screening workflows, and reproducible research artifacts.

I build Nexus Scholar as an open research-software stack: reusable PHP packages, Laravel host applications, command-line review workflows, graph tooling, and benchmark-safe documentation for research projects.

## Current Focus

- `nexus-scholar/core`: the reusable Laravel/PHP engine for scholarly search, deduplication, screening, full-text retrieval, citation graphs, and export auditing.
- `nexus-scholar/nexus-cli`: a local Artisan workspace for auditable command-line review workflows.
- `nexus-scholar/nexus-web`: the hosted Laravel/Inertia product shell around the Nexus Scholar workflow.
- `nexus-scholar/graph-core` and `nexus-scholar/graph-algorithms`: PHP graph packages for citation-network and general graph analysis.
- `nexus-scholar/refmanager`: bibliographic import/export support for RIS, BibTeX, CSL-JSON, and EndNote XML.

## Research Direction

My PhD work is in plant-disease AI and computer vision. Public research notes here are kept deliberately conservative: broad enough to show direction and evidence, but careful about unpublished methods, supervisor-sensitive details, and claims that still need validation.

I am also interested in AI-assisted academic workflows: systematic literature review tooling, screening support, citation-network analysis, document pipelines, and reproducible research software.

## Engineering Direction

Most of my current engineering work is Laravel/PHP backend development, research workflow systems, package design, API integrations, and research-facing SaaS/product infrastructure.

## Public Boundary

Open-source packages and technical artifacts are public when they are useful for demonstration, collaboration, or reuse. Hosted product details, private roadmap decisions, credentials, unpublished thesis methods, and sensitive experiment planning stay out of public repositories.
